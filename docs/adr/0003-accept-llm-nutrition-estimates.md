# Accept LLM nutrition estimates from explicit food quantities

The user accepts nutritional estimates generated by an LLM from recipe information and explicit ingredient quantities, such as 20 g of walnuts. Forkcast therefore does not require every ingredient to be matched to a food database before nutritional information can be available. This favors an effortless recipe workflow over requiring externally verified nutritional values; the resulting values remain estimates.

Recipe nutrition can also be entered manually or left blank. A person may log recipes with missing nutrition; daily totals sum known values and clearly indicate incompleteness. Editing recipe ingredients retains the existing nutrition and suggests AI reestimation when AI is configured.
