# Preserve existing records when archiving recipes

Archiving withdraws a recipe from the active collection and removes its future planned cooking entries. It is no longer offered for new planning or food-log entries. Past food-log entries retain their nutrition-version references, but cannot navigate to the archived recipe.

Reviewed shopping lists retain their items, quantities, and recipe contributions. The contributing recipe is shown as archived, and changes to the source meal plan are indicated without regenerating the list automatically. Already-started cooking sessions may finish from their captured contents with an archived notice.

These limited retained references preserve recorded consumption and work already reviewed or underway without making the archived recipe generally available for use.
