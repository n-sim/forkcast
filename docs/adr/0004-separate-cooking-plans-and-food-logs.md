# Keep cooking plans and food logs independent

The meal plan schedules which recipes to cook, on which days, and in how many portions; the food log records when and how much a person actually ate. Recipe data supplies nutrition for logging, and recent cooking activity can inform quick suggestions, but cooking does not automatically create consumption entries. Forkcast does not maintain an inventory of available or leftover portions, favoring low-effort planning and independent intake tracking over managing cooked-food stock.
