# Review shopping requirements without maintaining a pantry

A shopping list is generated from the recipe ingredients and planned portions within a selected meal-plan date range. The person reviews the resulting ingredient quantities and removes items or edits amounts based on what they need to buy, rather than maintaining a pantry inventory. The reviewed shopping list supports a PDF, an in-application checklist, and a Gurkerl MCP cart handoff; these are alternative ways to use the list rather than requiring a supermarket connection.

People may also add food items manually. The review before handoff shows these manual shopping items in a separate section. The shopping-list view also shows which recipes contributed to the generated requirements. These recipe contributions remain visible when a source recipe is archived, with that recipe identified as archived; archiving does not alter the reviewed list's items or quantities.

A reviewed list remains stable when its meal plan changes. The application indicates changes within the list's selected date range and offers explicit regeneration. Matching ingredients with compatible or convertible units are combined automatically; AI estimates can be offered for uncertain conversions and require acceptance before use.

Explicit regeneration replaces the recipe-generated ingredient requirements and overwrites their previous item removals and quantity edits. Manual shopping items and their quantities are preserved. The person reviews the regenerated recipe requirements afresh.

PDF export and supermarket cart population are handoffs that leave the shopping list available. Completion is a separate action: checking off all items or explicitly marking the list complete. Gurkerl product choices are to be driven by its MCP functionality and an editable AI system prompt, for example preferring the cheapest or previously purchased items.
